# What is quality of experience?

Quality of experience, or QoE, is the measure of how users perceive the performance and usability of a service. It focuses on the real user experience rather than only network metrics.

---

## How QoE works

QoE looks at what the user feels, not just what the network measures. A link can be up and fast, but the service can still feel poor if delays or errors affect the session.

This makes QoE a broader concept than latency or throughput alone. It combines technical signals with user impact.

---

## QoE in network operations

Operators use QoE to understand whether a service is actually usable. It is common in voice, video, streaming, and interactive applications.

The value of QoE is that it helps translate technical issues into business impact. That makes it easier to prioritize fixes.

---

## Common QoE signals

| Signal | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Latency | Delay experienced by users |
| Jitter | Variation in delay |
| Packet loss | Missing data affects experience |
| Buffering | Playback or delivery pauses |

---

## What makes QoE useful

QoE is useful because raw network health does not always match user satisfaction. A service may be technically available but still frustrating to use.

It is strongest when combined with application and traffic analysis.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is quality of experience?

Quality of experience, or QoE, is the measure of how users perceive the performance and usability of a service. It focuses on the real user experience rather than only network metrics.

### Why is quality of experience important?

Quality of experience is important because a service can look healthy in metrics but still feel bad to users.

### What affects QoE?

QoE can be affected by latency, jitter, packet loss, buffering, errors, and application behavior.

### How is QoE used?

QoE is used to evaluate whether users are actually satisfied with performance.
