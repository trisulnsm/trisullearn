# What are Traffic Trends?

Traffic trends are recurring patterns and changes in network traffic observed over time.

They help organizations understand traffic growth, peak usage periods, application behavior changes, and unusual activity patterns.

Traffic trends turn network data into long-term insight.

---

## In Simple Terms

Traffic trends are like watching traffic on a highway every day.

One day tells you volume.

A month tells you patterns.

You begin to notice:

- rush hours  
- quiet periods  
- growing traffic  
- unusual spikes  

Networks behave the same way.

Patterns repeat.

Until they don’t.

And that’s when you investigate.

---

## Technical Explanation

Traffic trend analysis studies traffic metrics over time to identify patterns.

Common trend dimensions include:

- bandwidth usage  
- packet rate  
- flow rate  
- host activity  
- application usage  
- protocol behavior  
- destination activity  

Trend analysis helps detect:

- growth patterns  
- congestion periods  
- behavioral changes  
- anomalies  

This creates predictive visibility.

---

## How Traffic Trend Analysis Works

1. Traffic data is collected continuously  
2. Metrics are stored over time  
3. Data is grouped by time intervals  
4. Trends are visualized and analyzed  
5. Changes and anomalies are identified  

This helps understand traffic evolution.

## Traffic Trends vs Historical Analysis

| Feature | Traffic Trends | Historical Analysis |
|---|---|---|
| Focus | Patterns over time | Investigation of past data |

Traffic trends focus on pattern recognition.

Historical analysis is broader.

Trends are one slice of history.

---

## Traffic Trends vs Real-Time Analysis

| Feature | Traffic Trends | Real-Time Analysis |
|---|---|---|
| Focus | Long-term behavior | Current behavior |

Real-time shows the present.

Trends show the trajectory.

Direction matters.
