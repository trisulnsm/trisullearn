# What is Bandwidth Throttling?

Bandwidth throttling is the intentional reduction of available bandwidth for certain traffic, users, or applications to control network usage and prevent congestion.

It slows traffic rather than stopping it.

A softer punishment.

Still noticeable.

---

## In Simple Terms

Imagine a water pipe.

Instead of turning the water off, you partially close the valve.

Water still flows.

Just slower.

Bandwidth throttling works the same way.

Traffic continues.

At a reduced speed.

Enough to function.

Not enough to dominate.

---

## Technical Explanation

Bandwidth throttling limits the maximum bandwidth available to selected traffic flows.

It is commonly used to:

- prevent bandwidth abuse  
- control heavy applications  
- protect critical traffic  
- enforce fair usage policies  
- reduce congestion  

Throttling usually applies to:

- users  
- applications  
- protocols  
- devices  

Selective control.

Targeted consequences.

---

## How Bandwidth Throttling Works

1. Traffic is identified  
2. Throttling policies are applied  
3. Maximum bandwidth is defined  
4. Traffic exceeding the limit is slowed  
5. Controlled traffic continues at reduced speed  

Traffic survives.

Velocity suffers.

A compromise.

---

## How Bandwidth Throttling is Applied

Bandwidth throttling can be applied by:

### User-Based Throttling

Specific users get reduced bandwidth.

Useful for fair usage enforcement.

---

### Application-Based Throttling

Heavy applications are slowed.

Examples:

- streaming  
- backups  
- downloads  

Some traffic earns limits.

---

### Protocol-Based Throttling

Specific protocols are controlled.

Examples:

- HTTP  
- FTP  
- P2P  

Protocols reveal appetite.

---

### Device-Based Throttling

Specific endpoints are limited.

Useful for unmanaged devices.

---

### Time-Based Throttling

Traffic is throttled during peak hours.

Scarcity by schedule.

Very human.

## Bandwidth Throttling vs Rate Limiting

| Feature | Bandwidth Throttling | Rate Limiting |
|---|---|---|
| Focus | Speed reduction | Traffic cap enforcement |

Throttling slows traffic.

Rate limiting may block excess.

Subtle versus strict.

---

## Bandwidth Throttling vs Traffic Shaping

| Feature | Bandwidth Throttling | Traffic Shaping |
|---|---|---|
| Focus | Selective slowdown | Traffic flow smoothing |

Throttling reduces speed.

Shaping smooths delivery.

Control wears many hats.

---

## Bandwidth Throttling vs QoS

| Feature | Bandwidth Throttling | QoS |
|---|---|---|
| Focus | Selective bandwidth control | Overall traffic quality management |

Throttling is one QoS tool.

QoS is the larger system.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is bandwidth throttling?

It is intentionally slowing traffic by limiting bandwidth.

### Is throttling the same as rate limiting?

Not exactly. Throttling slows traffic, while rate limiting may block excess traffic.

### Why do ISPs throttle bandwidth?

To manage congestion and enforce fair usage.

### Is bandwidth throttling useful for enterprises?

Yes. It helps control non-critical traffic.
