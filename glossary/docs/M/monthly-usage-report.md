# What is a monthly usage report?

A monthly usage report summarizes network usage over a month, showing traffic volume, trends, top consumers, and key events. It is used for planning, review, and accountability.

---

## How monthly usage reports work

The report gathers traffic data over a fixed month and rolls it into useful summaries. These may include totals, peaks, protocol breakdowns, and top users.

The point is to turn continuous data into something people can review quickly. That makes it easier to compare one month with another.

---

## Monthly usage reports in operations

These reports are useful for capacity planning, internal reporting, and chargeback. They also help managers understand where network resources are going.

If usage grows steadily, the report can support budget or upgrade decisions. If usage changes suddenly, it can help explain why.

---

## Common report contents

| Item | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Total usage | Overall traffic volume |
| Peak usage | Busiest observed point |
| Top consumers | Biggest users of traffic |
| Trend summary | Month-to-month changes |

---

## What makes monthly usage reports useful

Monthly reports are valuable because they summarize history in a familiar time window. That makes them easier to share across teams.

They are also useful for spotting long-term trends that daily monitoring may miss.
