# What is digital forensics?

Digital forensics is the practice of collecting, preserving, and analyzing digital evidence to reconstruct events and support investigations.

---

## How digital forensics works

Digital forensics starts by identifying relevant evidence. That may include logs, packet captures, disk artifacts, memory data, or network records.

The evidence is then preserved and analyzed in a controlled way. The objective is to reconstruct events without altering the original material.

---

## Digital forensics in operations

Digital forensics is used after security incidents, disputes, and major operational failures. It helps teams understand how an event unfolded.

It is also useful when evidence quality matters. The process focuses on repeatability, documentation, and traceability.

---

## Common forensic sources

| Source | Use |
|---|---|
| Logs | Event timeline |
| Packets | Communication evidence |
| Files | System changes |
| System records | Host activity |

---

## What makes digital forensics useful

Digital forensics is useful because it turns raw digital traces into evidence. That makes it valuable for investigations and audits.

It depends on proper handling. If evidence is changed or lost, the investigation becomes weaker.
