# What is Ingress Traffic?

Ingress traffic is network traffic entering a network, device, or interface from another source.

It represents incoming data.

Traffic arriving.

For better or worse.

Usually both.

---

## In Simple Terms

Imagine a building entrance.

People entering are ingress.

People leaving are egress.

Networks work the same way.

Traffic entering is ingress traffic.

Traffic leaving is egress traffic.

Direction defines meaning.

Simple. Rarely respected.

---

## Technical Explanation

Ingress traffic refers to packets entering a monitored network point.

Ingress can occur at:

- routers  
- switches  
- firewalls  
- servers  
- interfaces  

Ingress traffic includes:

- user requests  
- application traffic  
- API calls  
- incoming emails  
- attack traffic  

Everything starts by arriving.

Even problems.

Especially problems.

---

## How Ingress Traffic Works

1. A source sends traffic  
2. Traffic arrives at a destination network  
3. Devices receive and process packets  
4. Traffic enters internal systems  

Ingress is the entry point of communication.

Arrival before action.
