# What is Flow Deduplication?

Flow deduplication is the process of detecting and removing duplicate network flow records so traffic analysis remains accurate.

It prevents overcounting traffic and misinterpreting behavior.

Because duplicate data looks important.

It often isn’t.

---

## In Simple Terms

Imagine receiving the same bank statement three times.

If you add all three together, your expenses look three times larger.

Wrong data.

Wrong conclusions.

Flow deduplication prevents that.

Multiple devices may report the same traffic.

Deduplication keeps one version.

Truth with less repetition.

A rare luxury.

---

## Technical Explanation

Flow deduplication identifies identical or overlapping flow records generated by multiple exporters or observation points.

Duplicate flows commonly occur because of:

- multi-point monitoring  
- redundant links  
- mirrored traffic  
- multiple collectors  
- overlapping sensors  

Deduplication compares attributes such as:

- source IP  
- destination IP  
- source port  
- destination port  
- protocol  
- timestamps  
- byte counts  

Matching records are merged or removed.

Accuracy survives.

Inflation does not.

---

## How Flow Deduplication Works

1. Flow records are collected  
2. Records are compared for similarity  
3. Duplicate flows are identified  
4. Duplicate entries are removed or merged  
5. Clean flow data is stored and analyzed  

Less noise.

Better truth.

A respectable trade.

## Why Duplicate Flows Happen

Duplicate flows happen because of:

### Multiple Exporters

More than one device exports the same flow.

Too many witnesses.

---

### SPAN or Mirror Traffic

Mirrored traffic creates repeated observations.

Useful and repetitive.

---

### Redundant Monitoring Paths

High-availability paths duplicate visibility.

Resilience costs clarity.

---

### Multi-Tenant Collection

Shared infrastructure creates overlapping flow exports.

Complexity scales duplication.

---

### Distributed Sensors

Different sensors observe the same traffic.

Many eyes, same event.
