# What is security auditing?

Security auditing is the process of reviewing systems, logs, settings, and activity to verify security controls and detect policy gaps or suspicious behavior.

---

## How security auditing works

Auditing compares actual behavior with expected policy or control requirements. That may include reviewing logs, configuration, access records, and alert history.

The goal is to confirm that security controls are in place and working. It also helps identify missing evidence or weak settings.

---

## Security auditing in operations

Security auditing is used for compliance, internal review, and readiness checks. It provides proof that important controls are being followed.

It is also useful after incidents. Audit records can show what happened before, during, and after a security event.

---

## Common audit areas

| Area | What it checks |
|---|---|
| Access control | Who can do what |
| Logging | Whether events are recorded |
| Firewall rules | Whether policy is enforced |
| Configuration | Whether settings are safe |

---

## What makes security auditing useful

Auditing is most useful when records are complete and easy to search. Missing logs can make it hard to prove what happened.

It also depends on clear policy. Without a reference point, it is difficult to tell whether behavior is acceptable.
