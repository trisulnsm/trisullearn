# What is subscriber analytics?

Subscriber analytics is the analysis of network usage and behavior at the subscriber level to understand consumption, experience, and service patterns.

---

## How subscriber analytics works

Traffic, session, and service data are associated with a subscriber identity or account. That data is then used to measure usage, quality, and behavior over time.

This helps operators move from general traffic views to customer-specific insights. Instead of seeing only aggregate demand, they can see how individual subscribers experience the network.

---

## Subscriber analytics in operations

Service providers use subscriber analytics to understand customer behavior, spot unusual patterns, and improve service quality. It is especially useful for identifying top users, poor experience segments, and recurring issues.

It also supports business decisions. Usage trends can guide planning, product design, and support priorities.

---

## Common subscriber views

| View | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Usage volume | How much traffic a subscriber uses |
| Application mix | Which services are used |
| Experience trend | Quality over time |
| Behavioral anomaly | Unusual activity |

---

## What makes subscriber analytics useful

Subscriber analytics is useful because it connects network behavior to real customers. That makes performance and usage data more actionable.

It is most effective when subscriber identity is accurate and stable. Weak attribution makes the analysis less reliable.
