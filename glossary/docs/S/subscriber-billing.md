# What is subscriber billing?

Subscriber billing is the process of charging customers based on their service usage, subscription plan, or account terms. It is a core function in telecom and ISP operations.

---

## How subscriber billing works

Billing systems collect usage information and combine it with the subscriber’s plan. The system then calculates charges based on rules, quotas, rates, or commitments.

Some plans are fixed monthly fees, while others depend on consumption. In many telecom cases, both account structure and usage records matter.

---

## Subscriber billing in network operations

Subscriber billing relies on accurate usage data. If traffic, session, or identity records are wrong, billing errors can occur.

This makes billing closely connected to network analytics and subscriber mapping. Operators need to know who used what service and when.

---

## Common billing inputs

| Input | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Usage record | Shows consumption |
| Subscriber ID | Identifies customer |
| Plan | Defines charges |
| Accounting data | Supports invoicing |

---

## What makes subscriber billing work in practice

Usage records must be accurate and time aligned. Small errors in measurement can lead to billing disputes.

Billing also needs traceability. Operators often need to explain why a charge was generated and what data supported it.
