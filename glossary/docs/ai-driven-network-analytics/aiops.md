# What is AIOps?

AIOps is the use of artificial intelligence and machine learning to automate and improve IT operations.

It helps detect anomalies, correlate events, predict failures, and accelerate incident response.

Because modern infrastructure produces too much operational data for humans to manually process.

And humans were already struggling.

---

## In Simple Terms

Imagine managing thousands of alarms.

Some matter.

Most do not.

AIOps helps filter the noise.

It finds patterns.

Identifies real problems.

And sometimes fixes them.

Like hiring a very fast analyst.

Without coffee breaks.

---

## Technical Explanation

AIOps combines AI, machine learning, analytics, and automation to analyze operational data from infrastructure and applications.

It works on telemetry such as:

- metrics  
- logs  
- traces  
- events  
- flow data  

AIOps helps with:

- anomaly detection  
- event correlation  
- root cause analysis  
- predictive alerts  
- automated remediation  

Traditional monitoring creates alerts.

AIOps tries to make sense of them.

A noble mission.

---

## How AIOps Works

1. Operational data is collected  
2. AI models analyze patterns  
3. Related alerts are correlated  
4. Root causes are identified  
5. Automated actions may be triggered  

Data enters.

Patterns emerge.

Machines decide.

Humans supervise nervously.
