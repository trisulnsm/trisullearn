# What is Predictive Analytics?

Predictive analytics uses historical data, machine learning, and statistical models to forecast future behavior and potential problems.

It helps organizations predict failures, traffic growth, and performance risks before they happen.

Because reacting late is expensive.

Prediction is cheaper.

Usually.

---

## In Simple Terms

Imagine noticing:

- traffic is growing every week  
- server load is increasing daily  
- latency spikes every Friday  

You can predict what happens next.

Predictive analytics does that automatically.

Patterns become forecasts.

Forecasts become preparation.

A rare case of planning ahead.

---

## Technical Explanation

Predictive analytics analyzes historical and live operational data to identify patterns and estimate future outcomes.

It commonly uses:

- historical traffic trends  
- performance baselines  
- seasonal patterns  
- resource consumption  
- anomaly history  

Techniques include:

- regression models  
- time-series forecasting  
- machine learning models  
- statistical trend analysis  

Predictive analytics helps forecast:

- capacity growth  
- outages  
- performance degradation  
- anomaly probability  
- infrastructure saturation  

History informs probability.

Probability informs action.

A sensible chain.

---

## How Predictive Analytics Works

1. Historical data is collected  
2. Patterns and trends are identified  
3. Predictive models are trained  
4. Future behavior is estimated  
5. Predictions drive operational decisions  

Past becomes model.

Model predicts future.

Humans act.

Sometimes.
