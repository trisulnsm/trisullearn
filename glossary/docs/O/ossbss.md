# What is OSS BSS?

OSS BSS refers to the operational support systems and business support systems used by telecom and service providers. These systems manage service operations, customer accounts, billing, and provisioning.

---

## How OSS BSS works

OSS handles the operational side of service delivery. It manages network elements, faults, inventory, and provisioning tasks.

BSS handles the business side. It manages customer accounts, billing, pricing, and commercial service records.

---

## OSS BSS in network operations

OSS BSS is the backbone of telecom service management. It connects network behavior with customer and billing processes.

When a service changes or a customer is billed, both OSS and BSS data may be involved. That is why they are often discussed together.

---

## OSS vs BSS

| Area | Focus |
|---|---|
| OSS | Operations and service delivery |
| BSS | Billing and customer management |
| Together | Full telecom service lifecycle |

---

## What makes OSS BSS useful

These systems work best when operational data and billing data stay synchronized. If they drift apart, customers may be charged incorrectly or services may be provisioned wrongly.

They also need clean interfaces with network analytics and usage systems. Accurate records make both operations and billing more reliable.
