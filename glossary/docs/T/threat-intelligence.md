# What is threat intelligence?

Threat intelligence is information about malicious actors, attack methods, indicators, and risk patterns that helps organizations detect and respond to threats.

---

## How threat intelligence works

Threat intelligence may come from security teams, vendors, or external feeds. It is used to enrich alerts and compare observed activity against known threat patterns.

The goal is to turn raw events into context. That helps analysts decide whether something is suspicious or clearly malicious.

---

## Threat intelligence in operations

Threat intelligence is used in security monitoring, incident response, and threat hunting. It helps teams recognize known indicators faster.

It also helps with prioritization. If activity matches a known bad pattern, it can be escalated more quickly.

---

## Common threat intelligence types

| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| IOC | Indicator of compromise |
| TTP | Tactics, techniques, procedures |
| Feed | Shared threat data |
| Enrichment | Extra context added to alerts |

---

## What makes threat intelligence useful

Threat intelligence is useful because it connects local observations to broader threat knowledge. That reduces uncertainty during investigation.

It works best when the intelligence is current and relevant to the environment.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is threat intelligence?
