# What is traffic pattern analysis?

Traffic pattern analysis is the study of recurring behavior in network traffic to identify trends, peaks, anomalies, and application use.

---

## How traffic pattern analysis works

Analysts look at traffic over time and compare it with past behavior. This shows daily cycles, weekly cycles, recurring events, and unusual bursts.

The value comes from comparing current traffic against a baseline. A pattern only stands out if you know what is normal.

---

## Traffic pattern analysis in operations

This kind of analysis helps with troubleshooting, planning, and anomaly detection. It can show whether a link is busy at predictable times or whether traffic changed without warning.

It also helps identify the main applications and hosts driving usage. That makes it easier to explain why traffic is changing.

---

## Common pattern outputs

| Output | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Baseline | Normal traffic level |
| Spike | Sudden increase in traffic |
| Cycle | Repeating traffic shape |
| Anomaly | Unexpected change |

---

## What makes traffic pattern analysis useful

Pattern analysis is useful because many network problems are not isolated events. They are part of a recurring shape or trend.

That makes historical visibility important. Without history, it is hard to tell whether something is new or expected.
