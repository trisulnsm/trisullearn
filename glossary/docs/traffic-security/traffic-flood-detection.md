# What is Traffic Flood Detection?

Traffic flood detection is the process of identifying sustained high-volume traffic designed to overwhelm network links, servers, or applications.

It helps detect denial-of-service and resource exhaustion attacks.

Because too much traffic stops being demand.

And starts being damage.

---

## In Simple Terms

Imagine a doorway.

A few people entering quickly?

Traffic spike.

Thousands pushing nonstop?

Traffic flood.

Networks experience the same thing.

Flood traffic overwhelms capacity.

Flood detection spots it early.

Before systems collapse.

Or customers complain.

Whichever comes first.

---

## Technical Explanation

Traffic flood detection monitors traffic for sustained high-volume activity that exceeds expected operational limits.

It commonly detects:

- high bandwidth floods  
- packet floods  
- connection floods  
- protocol floods  

Flood traffic often aims to:

- exhaust bandwidth  
- overload infrastructure  
- degrade application performance  
- cause outages  

Floods are about persistence.

Not just size.

Duration weaponized.

---

## How Traffic Flood Detection Works

1. Traffic is continuously monitored  
2. Normal baselines are established  
3. Sustained abnormal traffic volume is identified  
4. Flood signatures or threshold violations are detected  
5. Alerts or mitigation actions are triggered  

Short spikes may be harmless.

Floods persist.

Persistence changes risk.

---

## What Does Traffic Flood Detection Monitor?

Traffic flood detection monitors:

| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| Traffic Volume | Sustained bandwidth overload |
| Packet Rate | Continuous high PPS |
| Flow Count | Connection flood growth |
| Source Diversity | Large distributed traffic sources |
| Destination Saturation | Single-target overload |
| Protocol Distribution | Flood protocol abuse |

Floods leave patterns.

Patterns leave evidence.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is traffic flood detection?

It is detecting sustained high-volume traffic floods.

### Is traffic flood detection the same as traffic spike detection?

No. Floods are sustained. Spikes are sudden and may be short-lived.

### Is traffic flood detection useful for DDoS defense?

Yes. Flood attacks are a major DDoS attack type.

### Can legitimate traffic look like a flood?

Yes. Flash crowds may resemble attacks.
